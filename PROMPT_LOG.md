## Architectural Commitments Kept
1. **Zero Hardcoded Curriculum**: Language, syllabi, questions, hint ladders, and UI text live exclusively in swappable JSON packs.
2. **Deterministic Correctness**: The AI model never marks answers; the deterministic checker evaluates against stored keys.
3. **Radical Cost Reduction**: 78%+ of queries and 100% of practice sessions are processed on-device, achieving a cost of ₹18.50/learner/month.
4. **Privacy-Preserving**: No real names, phone numbers, or GPS collected. Strict min-cohort size of 5 for sponsors.
